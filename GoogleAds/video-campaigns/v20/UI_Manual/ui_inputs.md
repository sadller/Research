# Google Ads UI - Manual Video Campaign Creation

## Overview
Creating a Video Campaign via the Google Ads UI (front-end) is the standard method fully supported by Google. The UI dynamically changes required fields based on the chosen "Campaign Subtype" and "Goal".

Reference:
- [Google Ads Help - Create a Video Campaign](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6340491?hl=en)

## Step-by-Step UI Inputs

### 1. Objective / Goal Selection
- **Sales**
- **Leads**
- **Website traffic**
- **Product and brand consideration**
- **Brand awareness and reach**
- **Create a campaign without a goal's guidance**

### 2. Campaign Type Selection
- Select **Video**.

### 3. Campaign Subtype Selection (Depends on Goal)
- **Drive conversions:** Get more conversions with automated bidding. (Becomes "Video Action" campaign).
- **Custom video campaign:** Customize your own settings with skippable in-stream ads, bumper ads, or in-feed video ads.
- **Video reach campaign:** Get the most reach for your budget.
- **Ad sequence:** Tell a story by showing ads in a particular sequence.
- **Outstream:** Show ads on phones and tablets outside of YouTube.
- **Audio:** Reach audiences while they listen on YouTube.

### 4. General Settings
- **Campaign name:** Text input for the name.
- **Bid strategy:**
  - *Target CPV* (Cost Per View)
  - *Target CPM* (Cost Per Thousand Impressions)
  - *Maximize conversions*
  - *Target CPA* (Cost Per Action)
- **Budget and dates:**
  - Budget type: *Daily* or *Campaign Total*
  - Budget amount: Currency input.
  - Start date: Date picker.
  - End date: Date picker (optional for Daily, required for Total).
- **Networks:**
  - *YouTube search results*
  - *YouTube videos*
  - *Video partners on the Display Network* (Checkbox)
- **Locations:**
  - All countries and territories
  - Your local country
  - Enter another location (Search/Radius)
- **Languages:**
  - Select languages your customers speak.

### 5. Content Exclusions
- **Inventory type:**
  - *Expanded inventory*
  - *Standard inventory* (Default)
  - *Limited inventory*
- **Excluded types and labels:**
  - Embedded YouTube videos, Live streaming games, DL-G, DL-PG, DL-T, DL-MA, Not yet rated.

### 6. Additional Settings
- **Devices:** Target specific operating systems, device models, or networks.
- **Frequency capping:** Cap impression frequency or view frequency.
- **Ad schedule:** Select days and times to show ads.
- **Third-party measurement:** Add tracking templates.

### 7. Ad Group Creation
- **Ad group name:** Text input.
- **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Parental status, Household income.
- **Audience segments:** Search or browse custom segments, affinity, in-market, life events.
- **Keywords:** Enter keywords related to your video.
- **Topics:** Select topics to show ads on content about specific subjects.
- **Placements:** Target specific YouTube channels, YouTube videos, websites, apps.

### 8. Ad Creation (Requires a YouTube Video)
- **Your YouTube video:** Paste the URL of the YouTube video you want to use.
- **Video ad format:**
  - *Skippable in-stream ad*
  - *In-feed video ad*
  - *Bumper ad*
  - *Non-skippable in-stream ad*
- **Final URL:** Where people go after clicking the ad.
- **Display URL:** The URL shown in the ad.
- **Call-to-action:** Short text (e.g., "Learn More", "Buy Now").
- **Headline:** Short text.
- **Long headline:** Longer text (for specific formats).
- **Description:** Text describing the offer/video.
- **Companion banner:**
  - Auto-generate using channel banner (recommended).
  - Upload an image.
- **Ad name:** Text input.

### Required Asset Specifications
- Videos must be hosted on YouTube.
- Companion banners: JPG, GIF, or PNG (300x60). Max 150KB.
